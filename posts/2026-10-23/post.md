## A. Daily theme
- **Category:** A. Past aur Memories (with a touch of F. Healing)
- **Emotional topic:** "Kaash" mein atak jaana — kisi purane decision ya insaan ke liye regret karte-karte present ko kho dena.
- **Real-life experience:** Raat ko baar-baar woh baat replay karna jo us din keh di hoti, ya woh decision jo alag liya hota — aur isi beech ghar walon ke saath ki chai, doston ke calls, aaj ke chhote pal chupchaap guzar jaate hain.
- **Audience connect:** Jo log past decisions par regret karte hain ya kisi ko move on nahi kar paa rahe, unhe yeh guilt nahi deta. Yeh bas yaad dilata hai ki doobte suraj ke peeche aaj ke taare bhi hain.

## B. Authentic source
- **Quote:** "If you shed tears when you miss the sun, you also miss the stars."
- **Writer:** Rabindranath Tagore
- **Work:** *Stray Birds*, aphorism No. 6
- **Edition:** New York: The Macmillan Company, 1916. Tagore translated it from Bengali into English himself (no separate translator).
- **Source URL:** https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/6524 (plain text: https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/6524/pg6524.txt)
- **Verification:** VERIFIED. The wording matches the Project Gutenberg text of the 1916 Macmillan edition word for word. Checked on 2026-10-23.
- **Copyright/reuse:** Public domain. It was published in the US in 1916, and Tagore died in 1941, so his works are out of copyright in India and in life+70 countries. The Gutenberg licence covers only the ebook file and its branding, not the text itself. Willy Pogány's frontispiece illustration is NOT used.

**Hinglish meaning (my interpretation, not Tagore's words):**
"Agar tum doobe hue suraj ke liye hi rote rahoge, toh aasmaan mein aaye taare bhi nahi dekh paoge."

## C. Main poem — Original Writing — Echoes of Yesterday
*Inspired by the themes of Stray Birds by Rabindranath Tagore. This is not a quotation.*

> Main roz shaam usi khidki par baitha raha,
> jahan se maine woh suraj doobte dekha tha.
> Har baar socha — kaash woh thoda aur ruk jaata,
> kaash us din maine kuch aur keh diya hota.
>
> Aur isi "kaash" mein, chupchaap,
> Maa ki di hui chai thandi hoti rahi,
> doston ke phone bajte-bajte chup ho gaye,
> aur poori raat, taare mera intezaar karte rahe.

## D. Caption (~95 words)
Kuch log zindagi se chale jaate hain, kuch decisions waqt ke saath galat lagne lagte hain. Aur hum unhi lamhon ko baar-baar replay karte rehte hain — "kaash thoda aur ruk jaata", "kaash maine kuch aur kaha hota."

Regret karna galat nahi hai. Woh batata hai ki kuch humare liye maayne rakhta tha. Lekin jab hum sirf us doobe hue suraj ko dekhte rehte hain, toh aaj ki chhoti roshniyan — kisi apne ka intezaar, ek call, ek shaam — chupchaap guzar jaati hain.

Tagore ne ise ek line mein keh diya tha. Hum bas yaad dila rahe hain.

Aapke "kaash" ne aapse aaj kya chheena hai?

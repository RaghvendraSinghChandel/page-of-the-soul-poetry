## C. Kavita — Mool Rachna — page_of_the__soul
**Adhure Jawab Aur Sukoon**

> Unke paas koi jawab nahi tha,
> aur mere paas ek aakhri sawaal.
> Phone ki screen par unke shabdon ka
> intezar karte-karte saal beet gaye.
> Ab samajh aaya ki wo khamoshi hi
> unka aakhri jawab thi.
> Bina kisi maafi ke bhi dil ko
> ab sukoon se jeena sikhaya hai.

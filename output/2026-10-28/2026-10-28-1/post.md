## C. Kavita — Mool Rachna — page_of_the__soul
**Bheed Mein Khamoshi**

> Sabhi baithe the ek hi mez par,
> Baatein thi, chai ki chuskiyan thi.
> Sabke paas kehne ko bahut kuch tha,
> Bas meri hi awaaz kahin gum thi.
> Unki hansi mein shamil toh tha main,
> Par andar ek ajeeb sa sannaata tha.
> Kitna ajeeb lagta hai yeh daur,
> Jahan bheed mein bhi dil tanha tha.

**Beeta Waqt Aur Aaj**

> Khidki par rakhi chai thandi ho gayi,
> Dhalati shaam ne purana panna khol diya.
> Pehle in yaadon se dil bhar aata tha,
> Aaj sirf ek halki muskaan aati hai.
> Waqt ne sikhaya ki har ek mod,
> Manzil tak pahunchne ke liye nahi hota.
> Kuch safar bas seekh dene aate hain.
> Ateet se ab koi gila nahi mujhe,
> Maine aaj ke sukoon ko chun liya hai.

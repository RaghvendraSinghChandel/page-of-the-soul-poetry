## C. Kavita — Mool Rachna — page_of_the__soul
**Sab Sambhalne Ka Bojh**

> Har kaam waqt par poora kiya,
> har mushkil ko akele hi seha.
> Kisi se madad maangne mein darr laga,
> ki kahin koi kamzor na samajh le.
> Sabne kaha tum toh sab sambhal lete ho,
> par shaam ko jab akele baithe,
> toh samajh aaya,
> mazboot dikhne ke bojh ne,
> saans lene ki jagah bhi nahi chhodi.

## C. Kavita — Mool Rachna — page_of_the__soul (prerna: Rahim ka doha)
**Ateet Ke Faisle**

> Ek purani photo ke peeche,
> ek purani tareekh likhi thi.
> Waqt toh aage nikal gaya,
> par woh faisla wahi ruk gaya.
> Aage badhne ki koshish ki,
> par woh mod wahi chhoot gaya.
> Aaj sab theek hai zindagi mein,
> phir bhi ek sawaal poochhta hai dil,
> agar tab thoda ruk gaye hote?

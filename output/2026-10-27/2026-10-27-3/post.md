**Khud Ki Talaash**

> Har kisi ki haan mein haan milayi,
> Apni har pasand peeche chhupayi.
> Dusron ke liye waqt nikaala,
> Par khud ke liye fursat na aayi.
> Aaj aayine mein khud ko jo dekha,
> Laga sabko sambhalte hum thak gaye.
> Ab thoda khud ka bhi hona hai,
> Yeh faisla ab humein hi lena hai.

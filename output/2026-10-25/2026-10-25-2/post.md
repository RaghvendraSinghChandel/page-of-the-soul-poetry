## C. Kavita — Mool Rachna — page_of_the__soul
**Bina Jawab Ke Aage Badhna**

> Phone par ek adhura message tha,
> jiska jawab kabhi aaya nahi.
> Kai raatein sawaal kiye dil se,
> par unka koi hisaab aaya nahi.
> Ab samjha ki unki khamoshi bhi,
> ek saaf aur aakhri jawab thi.
> Bina kisi maafi ke aage badhna,
> khud ko wapas paane ki shuruat thi.
